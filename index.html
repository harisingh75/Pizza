Our Story

Born in Janakpurdham,
Made for Everyone

Pizza Factory started with a simple dream — to bring authentic, restaurant-quality pizzas to the streets of Janakpur. Nestled near Tapshi Chowk, we've grown to become the city's most beloved pizza destination.

Every pizza that leaves our kitchen is made with hand-stretched dough, house-made tomato sauce, and the finest toppings — baked fresh to golden perfection. From classic Margheritas to our signature loaded specials, there's something for every craving.
